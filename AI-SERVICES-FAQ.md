## Frequently asked questions

### Who builds AI products in Mumbai?

Voltair Tech is an AI services studio based in Andheri West, Mumbai. The team works with Indian startups, enterprises, and global teams that need a fast Indian build partner, and is reachable on WhatsApp during business hours (IST).

### How much does an AI chatbot cost in India?

A production chatbot starts at ₹1.5L. A full RAG system or AI web app starts at ₹4–8L depending on scope, and mobile apps land at ₹8–15L. All quotes are fixed-price and include 30 days of post-ship support.

### How fast can you deploy an AI website in India?

AI-powered marketing websites go live in 48 hours from kickoff, including design, copy, CMS, analytics, and a custom domain. Larger product apps and mobile builds take longer, with a fixed timeline given at the scoping call.

### What is a RAG system and when do I need one?

RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) grounds a large language model in your own data instead of letting it guess from training. Use it for accurate, citable answers from a fixed corpus — policies, manuals, product docs, patient FAQs — where every answer links back to a verifiable source.

### Do you build WhatsApp AI agents for small businesses in India?

Yes. WhatsApp Business API is one of the most-requested channels. Voltair Tech handles Meta verification, conversation flow design, payment integration, and grounding the agent on your own documents, with native support for Hindi, Marathi, and English on the same bot.

### Can AI be integrated into existing software without a rewrite?

Almost always, yes. LLM features — summarization, search, copilots, agents — get added to existing web apps, mobile apps, and internal tools without rewriting the stack. Common integration targets are CRMs, helpdesks, dashboards, and document workflows.

### Is a WhatsApp AI agent legal under India's DPDP Act?

Yes, when built with consent management and data-handling controls from the start. Voltair Tech's AI Compliance & RegTech service specifically covers DPDP Act compliance audits and consent management systems, alongside GST auto-filing and reconciliation.

### What industries does Voltair Tech build AI for?

E-commerce & D2C, Healthcare & Clinics, Financial Services, Real Estate, Education & EdTech, Logistics & Supply Chain, Legal & CA Firms, and Hospitality & Restaurants — eight-plus sectors, with AI shaped to each sector's actual workflow rather than a generic template.
